What EOF is

Stockton’s Educational Opportunity Fund (EOF) is a special admissions and support program for New Jersey students who come from educationally and economically disadvantaged backgrounds and have the drive to succeed in college. It is funded by New Jersey OSHE and Stockton University.

EOF is more than financial aid. Students also get academic help, financial guidance, and personal support like tutoring, advising, workshops, and counseling so they can get in, stay in, and graduate.

Key idea: EOF is a full support system, not just a grant.

Basic eligibility overview

  • Be from an educationally and economically disadvantaged background.
  • Be a New Jersey resident for at least 12 consecutive months before receiving EOF.
  • Apply to and be accepted by Stockton University.
  • Meet Stockton’s academic admission criteria.
  • Apply as a full-time undergraduate.
  • File the FAFSA or, for eligible undocumented/DACA students, the New Jersey Alternative Financial Aid Application.
  • Use Stockton’s FAFSA code: 009345.
  • Have family income and assets within the EOF limits for your household size.

First-time applicants should show EOF interest on the undergraduate application. Stockton says EOF applicants can usually apply test-optional, though some majors may still require scores.

2026–2027 EOF income and asset caps

“Gross income” means total family income before taxes. The asset cap is the limit on counted assets like savings, property, and similar resources. You must stay at or below both limits for your household size.

Freshman EOF admission process

  1. Explore EOF and decide whether it might fit you.
  2. Review Stockton’s EOF page and the state eligibility page.
  3. Check your household size, income, and assets against the EOF scale.
  4. Apply to Stockton University as a freshman.
  5. Complete the undergraduate application and mark your interest in EOF.
  6. Follow Stockton’s test-optional policy unless your intended major requires test scores.
  7. File the FAFSA or the New Jersey Alternative Financial Aid Application.
  8. Use Stockton’s FAFSA code: 009345.
  9. Send any extra verification documents Financial Aid asks for.
  10. Watch email and your portal for EOF questionnaires, document requests, and invitations.
  11. Complete all forms by the deadlines.
  12. Prepare for the required five-week residential Summer Academy.
  13. Complete the EOF acceptance packet forms and any related Financial Aid or Wellness Center paperwork.
  14. Start fall semester as a full-time EOF student.

Deposit Waiver

First-year EOF students do not pay acceptance deposits or housing deposits.

Transfer EOF admission process

Transfer students already in EOF elsewhere

  1. Submit Stockton’s transfer application.
  2. Send current transcripts from all high schools and colleges attended.
  3. File the FAFSA and provide your Student Aid Report (SAR).
  4. Submit Financial Aid Transcripts from previous schools if requested.
  5. Complete Stockton’s EOF Transfer Application.
  6. Ask your current EOF advisor to send the EOF Transfer Form to Stockton’s EOF office.
  7. Attend the EOF information session when invited.

Transfer students not yet in EOF

  1. Apply to Stockton and send transcripts.
  2. File the FAFSA or NJ Alternative Financial Aid Application.
  3. Indicate EOF interest and complete Stockton’s EOF transfer form.
  4. Work with EOF staff to confirm eligibility and next steps.
  5. Attend any required information session or orientation.

Transfer applicants still need NJ residency, disadvantaged-background status, full-time enrollment, and income/assets within the EOF limits.

Summer Academy

All first-year, full-time EOF students must complete a residential Summer Academy before their first fall semester. It is required, not optional.

Why it matters

  • Builds college-level academic skills.
  • Teaches study habits and self-discipline.
  • Helps students grow socially and personally.
  • Builds confidence before fall classes begin.

2026 schedule

  • Required overview: June 17, 2026, 3:30–5:00 PM via Zoom.
  • Summer Academy: Saturday, June 28 to Thursday, July 23, 2026.
  • Two on-campus weekends: 7/10–7/12 and 7/17–7/19.
  • Accuplacer testing is taken online before the overview date, with limited on-campus seating.
  • New Student Orientation is June 29, 2026, and EOF staff register students.

Mandatory Requirement

Completing Summer Academy is a condition of joining Stockton as an EOF student.

Required forms and documents

  • EOF acceptance packet forms, completed and returned to your EOF campus program.
  • FAFSA or New Jersey Alternative Financial Aid Application.
  • Stockton FAFSA code: 009345.
  • Verification documents requested by Financial Aid.
  • Student Aid Report (SAR) for transfers.
  • Financial Aid Transcript (FAT) for transfers, if requested.
  • Stockton EOF Transfer Application.
  • EOF Transfer Form from your current EOF advisor, if transferring from another EOF school.
  • Any Wellness Center or other student services forms Stockton asks for.
Plan ahead: EOF students do not pay acceptance deposits or housing deposits.

What EOF expects after admission

  • Stay enrolled full-time, except rare situations like a final semester.
  • Maintain satisfactory academic progress.
  • Attend EOF meetings, advising sessions, workshops, and required activities.
  • Stay in good standing with the program.
  • Build community and connect with peers through EOF programming.

Some high-achievement groups linked to EOF, such as Chi Alpha Epsilon, may expect a 3.0 GPA, full-time status, good standing, and service hours. That is an example of extra achievement-level participation, not the basic EOF admission bar.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply without SAT or ACT scores?

Usually yes, under Stockton’s test-optional policy, unless your intended major requires scores.

Do first-year EOF students need Summer Academy?

Yes. It is mandatory for all first-year, full-time EOF students.

Do I have to pay a deposit?

No acceptance deposit or housing deposit is required for EOF students.

What should I do first?

Check eligibility, apply to Stockton, mark EOF interest, and file financial aid forms early.
